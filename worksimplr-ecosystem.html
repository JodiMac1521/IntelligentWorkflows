🎓 Phase 1 — Work While in School

Students start Sprint-terns during their academic journey, gaining real work experience alongside coursework.

  • Short-burst project work (2-6 week sprints) with real employers
  • Skill-matched assignments aligned to degree program
  • W-9 onboarding & tax fundamentals workshop (1099 basics)
  • Professional profile & digital portfolio creation
  • Mentorship pairing with industry professionals
  • Baseline data capture: skills, GPA band, demographic, goals

🤝 Phase 2 — Alumni Community

Graduates transition into a lifelong community modeled on university alumni networks, keeping them engaged and supported.

  • Automatic enrollment upon Sprint-tern completion
  • Peer networking circles & industry-specific cohorts
  • Annual check-ins: employment status, salary, satisfaction
  • Alumni spotlight stories & mentorship "give-back" program
  • Community forums, Slack/Discord channels, virtual meetups
  • Referral program: alumni refer new Sprint-terns

📚 Learning & Life-Skills Hub

Curated content that keeps members coming back — practical knowledge schools rarely teach.

  • New to Networking: LinkedIn optimization, cold outreach, informational interviews
  • W-9 & Taxes: Filing as a 1099 contractor, deductions, quarterly estimates, W-2 vs 1099
  • Financial Literacy: Budgeting, student loans, building credit, saving
  • Career Navigation: Negotiating offers, switching industries, upskilling
  • Free Online Courses: Curated links to Coursera, edX, Google Certs, HubSpot Academy, freeCodeCamp
  • AI & Tech Skills: Prompt engineering, no-code tools, data literacy

💼 Career Launchpad & Job Intelligence

Keep members on top of their careers with real-time labor market data and job board integrations.

  • Job Board Aggregator: Handshake, LinkedIn, Indeed, WayUp, RippleMatch
  • Lightcast Integration: Real-time labor market analytics, in-demand skills, salary benchmarks by region
  • Julius AI: Personalized job-market data visualizations & trend analysis
  • Skills Gap Alerts: "Your field is trending toward X — here's a free course"
  • Salary Transparency: Anonymous alumni salary data by role, industry, region
  • Resume & Interview AI Tools: Feedback bots, mock interview prep

🌎 Nonprofit & Industry Partner Interfaces

Strategic alignment with national organizations to ensure standards, credibility, research, and reach.

NACE — nace.org National Assoc. of Colleges & Employers: Competency frameworks, employer best practices, first-destination survey alignment, career-readiness standards
ASA — americanstaffing.net American Staffing Association: Staffing industry standards, legal/compliance guidance, workforce solutions research, credentialing
JFF — jff.org Jobs for the Future: Economic advancement research, equitable workforce policy, skills-based hiring advocacy, funder network access
  • Data Sharing: Aggregate anonymized outcomes data aligned to NACE first-destination survey & JFF impact metrics
  • Standards Alignment: Sprint-tern competency model mapped to NACE Career Readiness framework
  • Research Collaboration: Joint studies on gig-to-career pipelines, skills-based hiring, and economic mobility
  • Policy Advocacy: Partner with JFF on workforce policy; leverage ASA for staffing compliance

🔭 The Vision

Build the most comprehensive longitudinal dataset on early-career economic mobility — from first Sprint-tern to mid-career — while creating a self-sustaining community where every member gains tangible career value.
